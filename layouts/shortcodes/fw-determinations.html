Overall determinations in order of precedence
OrderDeterminationWhen it applies
1{{ index $l "fails_fundamental" }}Verified evidence shows that at least one fundamental eligibility requirement fails. Nothing else in the assessment can cancel this.
2{{ index $l "insufficient_evidence" }}A fundamental requirement is not verified, a principle is not assessed, or the evidence is incomplete. This is not a failure and not a pass.
3{{ index $l "meets_core" }}Every requirement passes, every principle is assessed as meeting, and the evidence is sufficient.
4{{ index $l "partially_aligned" }}Every requirement is established and nothing fails, but at least one principle is assessed as partially or not met.
