A curated index of software evaluated for how it treats user authority, records, and ongoing capabilities. This is an unscored qualitative catalog, not a ranking.

### A call for verification and independent evidence

Software sovereignty cannot be proven by marketing promises, documentation claims, or open-source licenses alone. Under our [evaluation framework](/methodology/), fundamental requirements (such as independent clean-machine recovery and governability of destructive operations) require direct, reproducible laboratory verification.

Because of this rigorous standard, most candidates currently hold an overall determination of **Insufficient evidence**. We actively invite researchers, engineers, and digital preservation practitioners to help test these applications:

- **Execute laboratory recovery tests:** Run clean-machine restoration trials (**T2**, **E3**) and cross-tool format migrations (**T1**, **E2**) in isolated environments.
- **Audit operational boundaries:** Verify undo, rollback, and authorization controls for destructive operations and sync conflicts (**T4**, **E5**).
- **Submit independent reviews:** Contribute parallel assessments following the [Two-Assessor Protocol](/methodology/#71-two-assessor-protocol-and-reassessment).

To contribute verification records, test fixtures, or new application entries, see our [contribution guidelines in the repository](https://github.com/polla-fattah/sovereign-software#call-for-verification-and-contribution).
