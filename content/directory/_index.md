Every entry in this index awaits assessment under [framework version 2.0](../methodology/). Until an entry has a complete, evidenced assessment, it is shown as **Unverified**, and no rating or classification is published for it.

Many entries were classified under the earlier framework, which used seven equally weighted tests. Those classifications are kept as labeled legacy history. They do not prove a score on any principle and are not current classifications. Descriptions, storage formats, and commands below are descriptive information, not tested observations. Entries are the maintainer's selection of software worth studying, not a ranking.
